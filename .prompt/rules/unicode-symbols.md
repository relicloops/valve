# Introduction

**IMPORTANT:** Reject/Replace emoji in docs/logs/source everywhere; use plain Unicode symbols.

### use of Emoticons


**General**
✓ check | ✗ cross | • bullet | ‣ bullet (alt) | ◦ bullet (alt)
∎ end marker | ⌁ wave | ⋯ ellipsis (midline)

**Arrows**
→ right | ← left | ↑ up | ↓ down | ↳ return/branch
⇢ right (alt) | ⇒ implies | ⇐ implies (rev) | ⇔ iff | ↔ bidirectional
⟶ long | ⟵ long (rev) | ⟷ long bidirectional

**Shapes**
□ empty square | ■ filled square | ◆ filled diamond | ◇ empty diamond
○ empty circle | ● filled circle | ❍ ring
▸ triangle right | ▾ triangle down | ▲ triangle up | ▼ triangle down (alt)

**Math / Logic**
≈ approximately | ≠ not equal | ≤ lte | ≥ gte | ± plus/minus
∞ infinity | ∅ empty set | ¬ logical not
∈ member of | ∉ not member of | ⊂ subset | ⊃ superset
⊕ circled plus | ⊗ circled times | ⊙ circled dot | ⊚ circled dot (alt) | ⊛ circled star | ⊜ circled equal

**Stars / Ornamental**
✦ star | ✧ star (outline) | ✶✷✸✹✺✻✼✽✾ star (alt)
✱ asterisk star | ✲✳ asterisk star (alt) | ❖ ornamental diamond
✿ floral | ❀❁❃ floral (alt) | ❂ sun
❄ snowflake | ❅❆ snowflake (alt)
❇ sparkle | ❈❉❊❋ sparkle (alt)
❦ floral heart | ❧ rotated floral heart | ❥ heart (ornate)

**Typography**
❘ light vertical | ❘̸ barred vertical | ❙ heavy vertical | ❚ heavy vertical (alt)
❛❜ single quote (ornate) | ❝❞ double quote (ornate)

**Card Suits**
♠ spade | ♥ heart | ♦ diamond | ♣ club
